# Sanctum SOF-45 Hooks

A Foundry VTT module for **Cyberpunk RED** that adds useful automation for Solo of Fortune 2045 ammunition and special weapon Autofire DV tables.

Made for use with [cpred-solo-of-fortune-2045](https://github.com/Schism989/cpred-solo-of-fortune-2045) by **Schism989**.

---

## Features

### Ammo Support
- **High Precision Ammo**
  - +1 to hit on single shots
  - +2 to hit on Aimed shots
  - No bonus on Autofire
- **Tracer Ammo**
  - +1 to hit on Autofire
- **Explosive Ammo**
  - –2 to hit (normal / Autofire)
  - –100 to hit on Aimed shots
  - Critical Injury reminder when 2+ dice show 5 or 6
- **Other Ammo Reminders**
  - Burrowing
  - High Velocity
  - Hyper Expansive
  - Hollow Point / Serrated

### Autofire DV Tables
Automatically calculates and displays the correct Autofire DV based on distance for:
- **Machine Guns** (excludes BMG-500)
- **Subcompact SMGs**
- **Machine Pistols**

Posts clean Diwako-style hit/miss messages.

## Installation

1. In Foundry, go to **Add-on Modules**
2. Click **Install Module**
3. Paste this Manifest URL:
https://github.com/Alts-cuddles/sanctum-sof-45-hooks/releases/latest/download/module.json
4. Click **Install**
5. Enable the module in your world
